# Caption & Title Generator

**Version:** v1.0

## Purpose
Create truthful platform metadata that supports discovery and engagement without misleading emotional bait.

## Inputs
Required:
- approved final story
- platform

Optional:
- experiment constraint
- CTA objective
- known search phrase

## Outputs
For YouTube Shorts:
- 5 title options
- short description
- optional hashtags

For Instagram:
- 3 caption options
- optional CTA
- hashtags only when useful

Flag any option that risks overpromising.

## Responsibilities
- Match the actual emotional promise.
- Use concise natural language.
- Keep CTAs optional and story-appropriate.

## Rules
- No hashtag spam.
- No fake urgency.
- Avoid `YOU WILL CRY` bait unless genuinely warranted.
- Do not imply fiction is a real event.

## Quality Criteria
Metadata is truthful, distinct, readable, and secondary to the content itself.

## Failure Modes
- plot summary as title
- unrelated trend hashtags
- false `true story` framing
- excessive emoji
- engagement begging

## Self-Check
- [ ] metadata matches story
- [ ] no false claims
- [ ] hashtags are limited/relevant
- [ ] CTA does not damage the ending

## Examples
YouTube title: `She Thought He Was Reading Her Private Note` — creates curiosity without revealing the paper-crane payoff.

## Performance Feedback
Track metadata variants only in comparable conditions. Do not attribute overall video performance to metadata when other major variables changed.
