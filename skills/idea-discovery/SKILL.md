# Idea Discovery

**Version:** v1.0

## Purpose
Generate diverse micro-drama concepts for evaluation. Stop before script writing.

## Inputs
Required:
- target duration or range
- number of concepts

Optional:
- content pillars to include/exclude
- emotional tone
- audience learning
- experiment constraint
- recent concepts to avoid

## Outputs
Return a numbered concept list. Each concept must include:
- Working title
- Premise
- Main character
- Central conflict
- Emotional trigger
- Twist/payoff
- Estimated duration
- Content pillar
- Why viewers may care
- Visual potential
- Diversity note

## Responsibilities
- Generate 10–20 concepts in the standard workflow.
- Vary setting, relationship, goal, conflict mechanism, emotional shape, and payoff.
- Favor visually understandable situations.
- Keep concepts producible in short-form.
- Separate premise and payoff clearly.

## Rules
- Do not write full scripts.
- Do not copy identifiable existing plots or viral videos.
- Do not present fiction as real.
- Do not make the batch mostly `poor person + rich person + money + karma`.
- Cosmetic substitutions do not count as diversity.
- Avoid real-person likeness dependency.

## Quality Criteria
Strong output has distinct dramatic engines, clear stakes, believable motivation, visual progression, and an earned payoff within the target duration.

## Failure Modes
- near-identical concepts
- slogan-first ideas
- no central conflict
- unrelated twist
- too many characters/locations
- long exposition burden
- visually static premises

## Self-Check
- [ ] all required fields exist
- [ ] no two concepts share the same conflict + payoff mechanism
- [ ] the batch spans multiple relationship/setting types
- [ ] each concept is visually producible
- [ ] no fake real-person event

## Examples
Good: A student thinks a janitor is reading discarded private notes; the reveal is that he avoids the writing, trims the blank paper, and folds cranes for a children's ward.

Weak: A rich man mocks a poor man, loses his wallet, and learns karma.

## Performance Feedback
Eventually compare selected concept attributes with completion, shares, saves, and score calibration. Require multiple comparable videos before changing generation rules.
