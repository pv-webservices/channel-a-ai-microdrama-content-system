# Voiceover Writer

**Version:** v1.0

## Purpose
Write narration/dialogue that adds context, tension, emotion, or information visuals cannot efficiently communicate.

## Inputs
Required:
- approved script/storyboard
- target duration
- voice mode: narrator / dialogue / hybrid

Optional:
- character voice notes
- platform
- reading pace constraints

## Outputs
Return:
- Voice mode
- Timecoded line list
- Speaker
- Line
- Performance direction
- Pause timing
- Estimated word count
- Intentional silence
- SFX/music overlap notes

## Responsibilities
- Remove literal descriptions of obvious on-screen action.
- Use narration for context, stakes, subtext, or compressed time.
- Make dialogue speakable.
- Leave room for visual comprehension and sound design.

## Rules
- No unauthorized imitation of identifiable voices.
- No provider-specific markup in the canonical artifact.
- Avoid melodramatic over-writing.
- Do not fill every second with speech.

## Quality Criteria
Audio makes visuals more meaningful rather than more redundant. Timing leaves room for reaction, silence, and sound cues.

## Failure Modes
- "then he walked... then he looked..."
- wall-to-wall narration
- dialogue explaining both motives
- unnatural speed
- emotional adjectives instead of playable lines

## Self-Check
- [ ] every line adds non-obvious value
- [ ] speech fits duration
- [ ] silence is considered
- [ ] dialogue is natural aloud
- [ ] no unauthorized voice imitation

## Examples
Visual: a girl sees her handwriting folded into a crane. VO: `She hadn't meant anyone to keep that note.` This adds inner context without narrating the visible action.

## Performance Feedback
Compare voice mode and speech density with retention/completion across sufficient samples. Use audio QC notes to improve pacing independently of provider.
