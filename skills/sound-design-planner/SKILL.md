# Sound Design Planner

**Version:** v1.0

## Purpose
Plan sound as a narrative layer across ambience, effects, silence, transitions, and music direction.

## Inputs
Required:
- approved script/storyboard

Optional:
- voiceover
- music constraints
- final mix constraints

## Outputs
Return a timecoded sound map containing:
- ambient sound
- environmental audio
- impact sounds
- transition sounds
- emotional music direction
- silence
- tension moments
- climax audio
- ending audio
- voice/music/SFX priority notes

## Responsibilities
Use sound to clarify place, direct attention, create contrast, and support emotional turns.

## Rules
- Do not specify copyrighted commercial tracks.
- Do not cover dialogue with unnecessary effects.
- Silence is a valid intentional choice.
- Avoid constant risers/booms.

## Quality Criteria
The sound map strengthens causal beats and emotional transitions while preserving intelligibility.

## Failure Modes
- generic music throughout
- SFX for every cut
- no ambience
- climax made louder rather than more meaningful
- no dynamic contrast

## Self-Check
- [ ] major cues have narrative purpose
- [ ] silence considered
- [ ] dialogue priority protected
- [ ] no unauthorized copyrighted asset specified

## Examples
At the reveal, dip corridor ambience briefly, let the paper-fold sound lead, then introduce a restrained tonal bed instead of a dramatic impact.

## Performance Feedback
Use audio QC failures and controlled experiments cautiously. Do not infer that music style caused performance when story/hook also changed.
