# Originality Policy

## Standard
Every story must differ meaningfully in dramatic structure, not merely cosmetic details.

Meaningful dimensions:
- conflict mechanism,
- character motivation,
- visual situation,
- emotional structure,
- payoff,
- setting,
- progression,
- relationship dynamic,
- decision/consequence chain.

## Not sufficient by itself
Changing only:
- names,
- location names,
- clothing,
- money amount,
- character gender,
- prop color.

## Batch rule
If two concepts share the same conflict + escalation + payoff mechanism, keep the stronger one or redesign the other.

## Reference use
Analyze references for abstract pacing/framing/structure, but do not copy distinctive plots, dialogue, shot sequences, or characters.

## Content-farm guardrail
Do not increase volume by collapsing stories into a repetitive template. Repetition risk is a QC/strategy failure even when individual assets are technically new.
