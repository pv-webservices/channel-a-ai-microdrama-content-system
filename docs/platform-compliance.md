# Platform Compliance

AI-assisted content still requires originality, rights discipline, and current-policy checks.

## Required principles
- Create original fictional stories.
- Use meaningful variation; avoid mass-produced repetitive templates.
- Respect copyright/licenses for music, images, footage, fonts, and other assets.
- Do not use stolen or unauthorized voices.
- Do not impersonate identifiable people.
- Do not fabricate events involving real identifiable people and present them as real.
- Follow platform synthetic-media disclosure requirements where applicable.
- Avoid misleading titles/captions.
- Avoid third-party watermarks and reused-content patterns.

Platform policies change. Before publishing automation or material workflow changes, verify current official YouTube and Meta requirements.
