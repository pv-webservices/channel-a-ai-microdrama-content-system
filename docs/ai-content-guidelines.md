# AI Content Guidelines

AI tools may assist ideation, writing, storyboarding, still-image generation, voice, sound planning, editing, and analysis.

## Channel-A visual-generation rule
- AI image generation is allowed.
- AI video generation is prohibited.
- Generated visual production assets must be still images/graphics.
- Remotion is the only video-construction layer for active productions.
- If a scene needs more visual progression, generate additional still keyframes and sequence/animate them in Remotion.

Requirements:
- keep human approval at defined gates,
- preserve traceability from concept to final artifact,
- use fictional characters unless a separate authorized real-person use case passes review,
- do not misrepresent synthetic events as documentary evidence,
- inspect image outputs for anatomy, identity, text, physics, and continuity defects,
- keep provider-specific syntax out of canonical story artifacts,
- treat generated content as requiring editorial judgment.
