# Experiments

Experiments reduce guesswork by isolating meaningful variables.

Prefer one major variable at a time where practical.

Suggested early tests:
- EXP-001 — question vs visual-mystery hook
- EXP-002 — ~30s vs ~45s story
- EXP-003 — narrator vs dialogue opening
- EXP-004 — realistic vs stylized visuals
- EXP-005 — fast-cut vs slower cinematic pacing

Do not call a result conclusive without enough samples to justify the decision.
