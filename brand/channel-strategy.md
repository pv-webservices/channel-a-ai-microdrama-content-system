# Channel Strategy

## Positioning
Original fictional emotional micro-dramas told through cinematic vertical video.

## Promise
Deliver a complete dramatic experience in under a minute: immediate tension, understandable human stakes, meaningful escalation, and an earned emotional payoff.

## Non-goals
- motivational quote videos,
- fake social experiments,
- fake stories about identifiable real people,
- repetitive rich-vs-poor karma variants,
- generic AI spectacle without story purpose.

## Creative moat
Build a reusable production system that improves story craft, continuity, pacing, and testing discipline using channel-owned evidence.
