# Visual Direction

## Default
9:16 vertical, mobile-readable, emotionally legible, cinematic but controlled.

## Priorities
- one dominant subject per important beat,
- clear visual hierarchy,
- believable environments,
- stable character identity,
- readable expressions,
- motivated camera movement,
- wardrobe/prop/time/geography continuity,
- lighting that supports emotion without becoming the subject.

## Avoid by default
- excessive orange/teal grading,
- glossy synthetic skin,
- oversaturation,
- random lens flare,
- generic luxury imagery,
- inconsistent architecture,
- impossible anatomy,
- meaningless slow motion,
- constant camera movement,
- cluttered frames,
- shallow focus that hides required story information.

Realistic or stylized looks are both acceptable when intentional and consistent.
