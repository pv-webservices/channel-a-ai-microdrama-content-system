# Storytelling Principles

1. Start with behavior, conflict, or a consequential image.
2. Make the viewer understand who wants what.
3. Escalate through decisions, not random events.
4. Let actions reveal character.
5. Withhold answers, not basic comprehension.
6. Make the payoff resolve the central tension.
7. Avoid moral lectures; let consequences carry meaning.
8. Keep exposition below the amount needed for clarity.
9. Prefer reversals that recontextualize earlier beats over arbitrary twists.
10. End as soon as the emotional promise is fulfilled.
