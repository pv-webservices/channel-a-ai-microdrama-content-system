# Audience Profile

## Initial audience hypothesis
Mobile-first viewers who consume short-form narrative content and respond to recognizable human conflicts, emotional reversals, and visually understandable consequences.

This is a hypothesis, not a demographic fact.

## Needs the content should serve
- understand the premise immediately,
- care what happens next,
- follow the story with or without perfect audio,
- receive an earned payoff,
- feel the story is distinct from mass-produced template content.

Do not hard-code demographic assumptions until analytics provide evidence.
