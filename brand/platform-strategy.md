# Platform Strategy

## YouTube Shorts
Prioritize immediate comprehension, strong watch progression, satisfying endings, and accurate titles. Descriptions and hashtags are secondary.

## Instagram Reels
Prioritize first-frame strength, mobile readability, share/save potential, and captions that add context without restating the whole plot.

## Shared default
- 9:16 vertical,
- 20–60 seconds typical,
- platform-safe caption placement,
- no third-party watermark,
- manual publishing in Phase 1.

Platform rules change. Verify current official requirements before operational publishing decisions.
