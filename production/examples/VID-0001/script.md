# VID-0001 Script

**Story ID:** STORY-0001  
**Target:** 38s  
**Selected hook:** H1  
**Voice mode:** hybrid

## Story objective
Turn a suspicious privacy violation into a visually earned act of kindness without lecturing the viewer.

## Beat structure
| Beat | Time | Event |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | Janitor reaches for Nia's discarded note; she pulls it back. |
| Situation | 3–8s | She notices several handwritten sheets on his cart. |
| Conflict | 8–13s | She accuses him of reading students' notes. |
| Escalation | 13–20s | He silently brings her to an art-room worktable and opens a box. |
| Turning point | 20–27s | He keeps writing face-down, trims inked sections, folds only blank paper. |
| Payoff | 27–34s | She sees dozens of cranes and a children's-ward donation card. |
| Resolution | 34–38s | She tears the blank half from her note and offers it. They fold together. |

## Timecoded script
| Time | Visual | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Nia holds a crumpled note above a bin. A gloved hand reaches in; she snaps it back. | NIA: "Why are you taking that?" |
| 3–8 | Her eyes shift to the janitor's cart: handwritten pages in a shallow tray. | Corridor ambience. |
| 8–13 | She steps between him and the cart, holding up her note. | NIA: "Do you read these?" |
| 13–20 | He gestures toward the nearby art room and opens a small storage box. | Cart wheel; room tone. |
| 20–27 | He turns the writing face-down, trims the inked strip, and folds only the blank remainder. | Paper cut/folds. NIA VO: "He never even turned the words toward himself." |
| 27–34 | He places the crane into a box filled with dozens more. Card: `Children's Ward — Friday`. | Restrained music enters. |
| 34–38 | Nia tears off the blank half of her note, slides it toward him, and begins a fold with him. | No narration. Paper crease. |

25.

- Art room is adjacent to the corridor.
