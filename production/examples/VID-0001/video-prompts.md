# VID-0001 Video Prompts

## SHOT-01 — 3s
Start: Nia's note hovers above the bin; Arun's gloved hand enters from right. Hand approaches; Nia notices, tightens grip, and pulls the paper back 15–20 cm; brows contract. Camera static. End with both hands visible and note protected against Nia's torso. Avoid hand morphing, aggressive lunge, camera shake.

## SHOT-02 — 5s
Nia's eyes move from her note to the cart. Cut to tray of crumpled papers; one loose corner shifts from cart movement. Keep cart on the same screen side.

## SHOT-03 — 5s
Nia steps half a pace into the cart's path and raises the note. Arun stops, glances at the note, then gestures once toward the art-room door. Camera mostly locked.

## SHOT-04 — 5s
Match from Arun's gesture to his gloved hands opening the storage box at the table. No walking sequence. End with blank paper squares visible.

## SHOT-05 — 9s
Three controlled beats: rotate paper with writing face-down; trim the inked strip; fold the blank section through two clear crease steps. Stable anatomy, no morphing/time-lapse.

## SHOT-06 — 7s
Arun places the crane into the box. Camera makes a subtle tilt/rack from cranes/card to Nia's reaction. Her expression moves from tense to embarrassed/softened, not sudden smiling.

## SHOT-07 — 4s
Nia tears along the blank half, slides the clean piece forward; Arun steadies it; their hands begin the first fold. End on one crisp crease.
