# VID-0001 / STORY-0001 — Hook Development

**Title:** The Flooded Box  
**Target duration:** 22–28 seconds  
**Status:** Stage 4 complete — H1 selected for the working script draft

## H1 — Immediate impossible choice
- **Hook type:** Immediate conflict
- **First visible frame:** Water races across a basement floor toward two objects in opposite directions: a new game console on a low shelf and a worn cardboard memory box.
- **First action:** The older brother runs in, stops between them, and looks from one to the other.
- **Spoken line:** “He had time to save only one.”
- **Unanswered question:** Which one will he choose, and why?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The choice must reveal something emotionally meaningful about him and his relationship with his sister.
- **Risk note:** Both objects must be legible within the first second. Do not clutter the frame.
- **Assessment:** Strongest balance of clarity, emotion, and immediate visual stakes.

## H2 — Value contradiction
- **Hook type:** Contradiction
- **First visible frame:** The expensive console and battered cardboard box sit inches above spreading water.
- **First action:** His hand moves toward the console, then stops over the box.
- **Spoken line:** “The cheap box was worth more.”
- **Unanswered question:** Why would a cardboard box matter more than the console?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The box contents must make that emotional value obvious without exposition.
- **Risk note:** Slightly more explanatory than H1 and risks telling viewers what to feel too early.

## H3 — Action-first
- **Hook type:** Shocking action / immediate movement
- **First visible frame:** A pipe leak sends water across the concrete floor.
- **First action:** The brother slides into frame and grabs the cardboard box while leaving the console behind.
- **Spoken line:** None.
- **Unanswered question:** Why is he saving the box instead of the console?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The box must reveal a believable reason for the choice.
- **Risk note:** Shows the decision too early, reducing suspense around which object he will choose.

## H4 — Object mystery
- **Hook type:** Visual mystery
- **First visible frame:** A child’s drawing sticks halfway out of a damp cardboard box while water approaches.
- **First action:** The brother reaches past an expensive console toward the drawing.
- **Spoken line:** None.
- **Unanswered question:** What is inside the box, and why does it matter to him?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The sibling connection must become clear.
- **Risk note:** Strong emotion but weaker immediate “two-choice” clarity than H1.

## H5 — Consequence-first
- **Hook type:** Unresolved outcome
- **First visible frame:** The younger sister stares at a water-damaged console on the basement floor.
- **First action:** Her eyes shift to the dry cardboard box in her brother’s arms.
- **Spoken line:** “She knew what he chose.”
- **Unanswered question:** What happened, and why did he choose the box?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The flashback must explain the choice quickly.
- **Risk note:** Nonlinear structure adds complexity to a very short story and partially reveals the payoff.

## H6 — Silent binary choice
- **Hook type:** Visual conflict
- **First visible frame:** Split visual relationship: console left, memory box right, water spreading between them.
- **First action:** The brother enters the center of frame and freezes.
- **Spoken line:** None.
- **On-screen text:** “ONLY ONE.”
- **Unanswered question:** What does he save?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The choice must feel emotionally earned.
- **Risk note:** Text dependence is slightly higher than H1, though the image still works muted.

## H7 — Emotional object trigger
- **Hook type:** Emotional statement / visual reveal
- **First visible frame:** A wet childhood drawing slips from the memory box onto the floor.
- **First action:** The brother notices it while already reaching for the console.
- **Spoken line:** “Then he saw what was inside.”
- **Unanswered question:** What changed his decision?
- **Promise the story must fulfill:** The drawing must connect clearly to his younger sister.
- **Risk note:** Starts one beat later in the conflict and is less instantly legible than H1.

## Ranked top 3
1. **H1 — Immediate impossible choice**
2. **H6 — Silent binary choice**
3. **H2 — Value contradiction**

## Working selection
**H1 — “He had time to save only one.”**

H1 is selected for the working script because it creates one clear question immediately, works visually on a phone screen, does not reveal the payoff, and requires only seven spoken words.

This hook remains editable until the Phase B Human Script Approval gate.
