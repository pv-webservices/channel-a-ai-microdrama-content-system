# VID-0001 / STORY-0001 — Concept

**Working title:** The Flooded Box  
**Selected concept ID:** C02  
**Selection date:** 2026-09-09  
**Selection status:** HUMAN APPROVED — PHASE A COMPLETE  
**Target duration:** 22–28 seconds  
**Target platforms:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels  
**Content pillar:** siblings / sacrifice / difficult choices

## Premise
A pipe bursts in a basement storage room and a 17-year-old boy has seconds to choose between saving his new game console and a cardboard box filled with his younger sister’s childhood drawings, school notes, and handmade cards.

## Main character
A 17-year-old older brother.

## Central conflict
Rising water makes it impossible to carry both items at once.

## Emotional trigger
The console is expensive and replaceable. The cardboard box looks cheap but contains years of irreplaceable memories belonging to his younger sister.

## Payoff
He chooses the cardboard box and leaves the console behind. Later, his sister sees the ruined console and understands what he chose without needing an explanation.

## Why this concept was selected
- immediate visual conflict,
- minimal exposition,
- clear irreversible choice,
- strong sibling emotion,
- payoff carried through behavior rather than a moral speech,
- manageable number of characters and locations,
- strong image/video/sound-design potential,
- suitable for a first full Remotion production test.

## Production cautions
- Keep the basement/water setup visually believable without turning the story into disaster spectacle.
- Avoid unsafe electrical imagery; the console should be visibly disconnected/unpowered before water reaches it.
- Do not over-explain why the memory box matters; selected contents should make that clear visually.
- The sister’s reaction should complete the emotional payoff without dialogue explaining the sacrifice.
