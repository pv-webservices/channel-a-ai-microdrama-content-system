# CHAR-001 — Older Brother

## Role
Primary protagonist. He makes the central irreversible choice.

## Approximate age
17

## Stable physical characteristics
- warm medium skin tone
- dark brown eyes
- straight dark-brown eyebrows
- short, slightly wavy dark-brown hair
- clean-shaven
- lean teenage build
- slightly taller than CHAR-002
- natural, non-model-like appearance

## Face / Features
- oval-to-rectangular face
- medium-width nose
- defined but youthful jawline
- expressive eyes
- subtle under-eye tiredness is acceptable
- no facial hair
- no facial piercings

## Hairstyle / Color
- short dark-brown hair
- slightly wavy texture
- longer on top, shorter at sides
- natural, slightly messy from rushing
- hair length and part must remain consistent across every shot

## Body / Build
- lean, average-height-to-tall teenage build
- believable 17-year-old proportions
- no exaggerated athletic physique

## Default wardrobe
**Lock for entire story:**
- charcoal-gray zip hoodie, open
- faded olive-green crew-neck T-shirt
- dark indigo jeans
- black low-top canvas sneakers
- simple black digital watch on left wrist

## Allowed wardrobe changes
None during this story.

Wetness may increase slightly at lower jeans/shoes as water exposure progresses, but:
- hoodie color does not change
- shirt color does not change
- watch remains on left wrist
- shoes remain black canvas sneakers

## Accessories
- black digital watch, left wrist only
- no necklace
- no hat
- no glasses
- no backpack

## Default expressions
- focused
- mildly tense
- quick visual assessment
- emotionally restrained

## Emotional range
Allowed:
- urgency
- hesitation
- realization
- quiet resolve
- restrained tenderness

Avoid:
- exaggerated panic
- sobbing
- theatrical shock
- broad smiling

## Distinctive movement / Behavior
- moves quickly but decisively
- first instinct is toward the console
- decision is shown through eye movement rather than speech
- protects the loose drawing against the box after choosing it
- final shrug is extremely small and understated

## Continuity lock
The following must remain stable across all generated images/video:
- face shape
- eye color
- eyebrow shape
- hair length/texture
- hoodie + olive T-shirt
- black watch on left wrist
- dark jeans
- black canvas sneakers
- lean build
- age appearance: clearly teenage, approximately 17

## Prohibited drift
Do not:
- age him into his 20s
- add beard/stubble
- change hair to curly/long/blond
- change hoodie color
- remove or switch the watch wrist
- make him muscular/bodybuilder-like
- add jewelry/tattoos
- change ethnicity/skin tone between scenes
- use a celebrity likeness
- make him look fashion-editorial or overly polished

## Concise prompt identity block
**CHAR-001:** 17-year-old older brother, warm medium skin, dark brown eyes, short slightly wavy dark-brown hair, lean teenage build, oval-rectangular youthful face, charcoal-gray open zip hoodie over faded olive-green T-shirt, dark indigo jeans, black low-top canvas sneakers, simple black digital watch on left wrist; restrained expressive face, natural realistic appearance; lock face, hair, hoodie, shirt, watch, jeans and shoes across all shots.

# Sibling Relationship Continuity

## Visual resemblance
The characters should plausibly read as siblings through:
- similar dark brown eye color
- similar eyebrow shape
- related warm medium skin tone
- compatible facial proportions

Do not make them visually identical.

## Height relationship
- CHAR-001 is clearly taller
- CHAR-002 reaches approximately upper-chest / shoulder level relative to CHAR-001 depending on camera angle

## Interaction style
Their relationship should feel familiar and understated:
- no melodramatic physical affection
- no forced smiles
- small eye contact carries emotional meaning
- CHAR-001’s final shrug should feel ordinary/familial
- CHAR-002’s understanding is expressed through gaze and action

# Continuity-Critical Story Props

These are not characters, but their consistency strongly affects character continuity.

## PROP-001 — Memory Box
- worn medium-size brown corrugated cardboard box
- hand-written marker label is optional but should not be required for comprehension
- softened lower corners from moisture
- still structurally carryable at the decision point
- same dents, tape seams and proportions across shots

## PROP-002 — Sibling Drawing
- child-made drawing on white paper
- clearly depicts two simplified sibling figures holding hands
- simple bright crayon colors
- same drawing design across reveal and ending
- no tiny text required

## PROP-003 — Game Console
- modern but brand-neutral matte-black game console
- matching controller
- clearly unplugged power cable visible near shelf / disconnected
- same orientation/location after CHAR-001 releases it
- no visible real-world brand logo
