# VID-0001 / STORY-0001 — Script

**Title:** The Flooded Box  
**Script version:** v1 — pre-retention review  
**Target runtime:** ~26 seconds  
**Selected working hook:** H1 — Immediate impossible choice  
**Voice mode:** Minimal narrator + visual storytelling

## 1. Story objective
Make the audience understand, through one irreversible choice, that the brother values his younger sister’s irreplaceable memories more than his own expensive possession—without explaining the moral.

## 2. Beat table

| Beat | Time | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:03 | Present two threatened objects and one impossible choice. |
| Situation | 0:03–0:06 | Establish water is spreading and the memory box is beginning to get wet. |
| Conflict | 0:06–0:10 | Brother initially reaches for the console. |
| Escalation | 0:10–0:15 | A childhood drawing slips from the box; he changes course and saves the box. |
| Emotional turning point | 0:15–0:19 | Sister arrives and notices the abandoned, water-damaged console. |
| Payoff | 0:19–0:24 | She sees the contents he saved and understands his choice. |
| Resolution | 0:24–0:26 | Quiet sibling connection; end immediately. |

## 3. Timecoded script

### 0:00–0:03 — HOOK
**VISUAL:** Basement floor. Water spreads quickly across concrete. On one side: a new game console on a low storage shelf, its power cable visibly unplugged. On the other: a worn cardboard box marked with old stickers and a child’s handwriting. The older brother runs in and stops between them.

**AUDIO:** Water rush + pipe hiss.  
**VO:** “He had time to save only one.”

### 0:03–0:06 — SITUATION
**VISUAL:** The water reaches the bottom edge of the cardboard box. The cardboard darkens. The brother looks at the console, then the box.

**AUDIO:** Water intensifies. No narration.

### 0:06–0:10 — CONFLICT
**VISUAL:** He steps toward the console and puts both hands on it. As he starts to lift it, a loose childhood drawing slips from the damp box and lands face-up.

**AUDIO:** Console scrape. Wet paper slap on concrete.

### 0:10–0:15 — ESCALATION / CHOICE
**VISUAL:** Close enough to read the drawing as a young child’s picture of two siblings holding hands. He looks at it once, releases the console, grabs the memory box with both arms, and carries it toward the stairs. Water reaches the console shelf.

**AUDIO:** Cardboard creak, footsteps through shallow water. No dialogue.

### 0:15–0:19 — TURNING POINT
**VISUAL:** His younger sister appears at the basement doorway. She sees him carrying her box. Then her eyes move past him to the wet console he left behind.

**AUDIO:** Water sound drops slightly under room tone.

### 0:19–0:24 — PAYOFF
**VISUAL:** He sets the box on a dry step. The sister opens the top just enough to see old drawings, handmade cards, and childhood school papers. She picks up the same drawing. Her eyes return to the ruined console, then to her brother.

**AUDIO:** Paper movement. Very restrained emotional tonal bed may enter.

### 0:24–0:26 — RESOLUTION
**VISUAL:** She hugs the box against herself with one arm and reaches with the other to squeeze his wet hand. He exhales. Cut.

**AUDIO:** Water remains distant. No moral line. End.

## 4. Approximate spoken word count
7 spoken words total.

## 5. Continuity requirements
- Console must remain the same model/position throughout.
- Its power cable must be visibly unplugged before water reaches it.
- Memory box must retain the same worn stickers/markings.
- The same child drawing that slips out must reappear during the payoff.
- Brother’s clothing should visibly become wet only around shoes/lower legs, consistent with shallow water.
- Sister should enter from the dry stair/doorway side.
- Water level and wetness must progress consistently from shot to shot.

## 6. Risks / open questions
- The 0:03–0:06 setup may repeat information already visible in the hook.
- The drawing must read as a sibling memory without requiring legible text.
- The sister’s 0:19–0:24 inspection may be too long if the visual assets are static.
- Avoid making the ruined console look electrically dangerous; it is disconnected.
- The ending should remain restrained and stop immediately after understanding is shared.
