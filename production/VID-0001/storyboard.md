# Scene-by-Scene Production Notes

## SC-01 — Hook Frame

**Purpose:** communicate the entire decision architecture in under two seconds.

**Start state:**  
Water already present. CHAR-001 enters quickly.

**End state:**  
CHAR-001 stops between the two choices.

**Critical readable elements:**
1. CHAR-001
2. PROP-003 console, camera-left
3. PROP-001 box, camera-right
4. shallow advancing water
5. visible disconnected power cable / clearly unplugged state

**Do not:** open with pipe mechanics, exterior setup, or unrelated establishing shots.

---

## SC-02 — First Instinct

**Purpose:** prove his initial personal preference is the console.

The shot must make the reach physically immediate. Avoid a thoughtful pause before he touches the console; the hesitation belongs later after the drawing reveal.

**Continuity:**  
His left-wrist watch may appear depending on hand choice, but if visible it must remain black and on the left wrist.

---

## SC-03 — Memory Evidence

**Purpose:** give the audience one concrete reason the box is irreplaceable.

The child drawing is sufficient evidence. Additional cards/photos should remain secondary texture.

**PROP-002 lock established here:**  
Two simple sibling figures holding hands, bright basic crayon palette on white paper. This exact design returns in SC-07.

---

## SC-04 — Decision

**Purpose:** make the choice emotionally legible without narration.

Required eyeline:
1. drawing
2. box
3. console

Then release / resolve.

Keep the scene short. This is not a crying close-up.

---

## SC-05 — Choice in Action

**Purpose:** convert internal decision into irreversible physical action.

CHAR-001 must:
- leave PROP-003 where it is,
- lift PROP-001,
- protect PROP-002,
- move toward rear/right stairs.

The console remaining behind must still be visible long enough to register.

---

## SC-06 — Sister Realization

**Purpose:** deliver the payoff through CHAR-002 rather than narration.

Required gaze order:
1. console left behind
2. box in CHAR-001’s arms
3. CHAR-001

Do not reverse this order.

This scene needs the longest hold because the audience must read her understanding, but performance stays restrained.

---

## SC-07 — Resolution

**Purpose:** confirm what was saved and end immediately after emotional fulfillment.

No dialogue.  
No hug required.  
No replacement console.  
No moral statement.  
No logo/outro inside the story master.

End after:
drawing → brother → tiny shrug → step beside him.

# AI Generation Continuity Notes

## Character locks
Always reference:
- CHAR-001 from `characters.md`
- CHAR-002 from `characters.md`

Never redefine their appearance scene-by-scene.

## Environment lock
The basement should remain the same across scenes:
- unfinished concrete floor
- neutral painted or unfinished storage walls
- practical shelving
- low shelf containing console and box
- stairs rear/right
- no architecture redesign between shots

## Water lock
Keep water:
- shallow
- visually consistent in direction
- below dangerous depth
- free from electrical sparks
- free from dramatic wave behavior

## Prop locks
- PROP-001 same dents/tape seams/proportions
- PROP-002 same drawing design
- PROP-003 same matte-black brand-neutral console/controller
- console remains unplugged

## Prohibited visual drift
- mirrored room geography
- changing stair location
- console changing model/color
- cardboard box changing dimensions
- drawing changing artwork
- CHAR-001 age/wardrobe drift
- CHAR-002 age/wardrobe drift
- exaggerated tears/hugs
- cinematic disaster spectacle replacing grounded tension
