# VID-0001 / STORY-0001 — Retention Review

**Target duration:** 22–28 seconds  
**Reviewed script:** v1  
**Selected hook:** H1

## 1. Retention issues

### 0:03–0:06 — MEDIUM
The script repeats the hook’s existing information: water is spreading and both objects are threatened. Three extra seconds before the brother meaningfully commits to a choice may feel static.

### 0:10–0:15 — LOW
The drawing reveal works, but holding too long on a readable illustration risks making the edit feel explanatory. The drawing should be understood as a sibling memory in one quick visual impression.

### 0:15–0:19 — MEDIUM
The sister’s entrance followed by a separate look to the console creates a potentially slow reaction beat. Her arrival and recognition can overlap more tightly.

### 0:19–0:24 — MEDIUM
Opening the box, showing multiple contents, picking up the same drawing, looking at the console, and then looking at her brother creates too many micro-actions for five seconds.

### Visual execution risk — MEDIUM
The story has only one location and two principal objects. If every shot is a static close-up, visual change may feel limited despite the strong premise.

## 2. Recommended changes

1. Compress the setup so the brother reaches toward the console by ~0:03 rather than ~0:06.
2. Let the childhood drawing slip out during his first console reach; do not pause for a long insert.
3. Make the sister enter as he reaches the dry step, allowing her to see the box and abandoned console within the same continuous beat.
4. Reduce the payoff to one saved drawing/card cluster rather than an inventory of box contents.
5. End on one restrained physical gesture immediately after she understands.
6. Preserve the seven-word H1 narration only; do not add explanatory VO.

## 3. Revised beat structure

| Beat | Revised time | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:03 | Keep two-object choice and H1 VO. |
| Conflict | 0:03–0:07 | Brother immediately commits toward console; drawing slips out. |

Target runtime becomes approximately **25 seconds**.

## 4. Revised script

### 0:00–0:03 — HOOK
**VISUAL:** Water spreads across the basement floor toward a new game console on a low shelf and a worn cardboard memory box. The console’s power cable is visibly unplugged. The older brother runs into frame and stops between them.

**AUDIO:** Water rush + pipe hiss.  
**VO:** “He had time to save only one.”

### 0:03–0:07 — CONFLICT
**VISUAL:** He immediately grabs the console. As it lifts, a damp childhood drawing slips from the cardboard box and lands face-up near his shoe: two young siblings holding hands.

**AUDIO:** Console scrape + wet paper slap.

### 0:07–0:12 — CHOICE
**VISUAL:** He looks down once. He lowers the console, crosses to the box, and lifts it with both arms. Water reaches the console shelf as he turns toward the stairs.

**AUDIO:** Cardboard creak, footsteps through shallow water. No narration.

### 0:12–0:17 — TURNING POINT
**VISUAL:** His younger sister appears at the top of the basement stairs just as he reaches the first dry step. She sees her old box in his arms, then looks past him at the wet console below.

**AUDIO:** Water recedes slightly in the mix; room tone opens.

### 0:17–0:22 — PAYOFF
**VISUAL:** He sets the box down. The same childhood drawing sits on top beside a few handmade cards. She picks up the drawing, recognizes it, then looks back at the console and finally at him.

**AUDIO:** Paper rustle. A restrained tonal bed may enter.

### 0:22–0:25 — RESOLUTION
**VISUAL:** She pulls the box against her chest and reaches out to squeeze his wet hand. He exhales. Cut immediately.

**AUDIO:** Distant water only. No moral line.
