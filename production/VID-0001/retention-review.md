# VID-0001 / STORY-0001 — Retention Review

**Target duration:** 22–28 seconds  
**Reviewed script:** The Flooded Box  
**Selected hook:** H1 — “He had time to save only one.”

## 1. Retention Issues

| Time / beat | Severity | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 2–5s / first instinct | LOW | The console reach is necessary, but it must happen immediately; a slow approach would weaken the hook's time-pressure promise. |
| 5–10.5s / memory reveal + decision | MEDIUM | The emotional reveal can become overlong if the camera lingers on the drawing or the brother's face. The audience needs enough information to understand the box, then the choice should follow quickly. |
| 14.5–20.5s / sister payoff | MEDIUM | A separate “later” scene would reset momentum. Keeping the sister at the same basement stair location preserves causal continuity and avoids a second setup. |
| 20.5–25s / resolution | LOW | A hug, speech, or moral line would extend the video after the payoff and make the ending feel explained rather than felt. |

## 2. Recommended Changes

1. Make the console reach immediate at 2s; no approach/walking beat.
2. Limit the memory reveal to one clear child drawing plus visible handmade material in the box; do not montage multiple memories.
3. Compress the brother's decision to a fast three-point eyeline: drawing → box → console.
4. Keep the sister's arrival in the same basement/stair geography instead of cutting to a later room.
5. End on the sister understanding the choice; no hug requirement, no narrated moral, no reward outcome.
