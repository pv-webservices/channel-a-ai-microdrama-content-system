## C01 — The Camera Down
**Strengths:** contemporary consent theme, clean phone/stage visual, restrained family emotion.  
**Risks:** live-stream state must be legible without phone-text exposition.

## C02 — The Flooded Box
**Strengths:** strongest irreversible choice; minimal dialogue; highly visual; strong sibling payoff.  
**Risks:** water staging must stay simple and safe; avoid melodramatic danger.

## C03 — The Spare Helmet
**Strengths:** compact two-character story, easy object continuity, practical sacrifice.  
**Risks:** helmet damage must be readable; the older brother’s own sacrifice must register quickly.

## C04 — The Group Photo
**Strengths:** broad relatability, warm inclusion payoff, simple production, no villain.  
**Risks:** establish that the stepbrother has been excluded without repetitive setup.

## C05 — The Name Card
**Strengths:** actual betrayal, strong visual evidence, consequence without revenge.  
**Risks:** process-screen proof must read quickly; avoid cartoonish villain behavior.

## C06 — The Empty Carriage
**Strengths:** excellent curiosity, quiet kindness, minimal dialogue, strong social behavior.  
**Risks:** the uncomfortable background behavior must be clear without becoming melodramatic.

## C07 — The Closed Curtain
**Strengths:** exceptional visual clarity, immediate interruption hook, almost dialogue-free.  
**Risks:** emotional depth is lower than C02/C06; stage-safety reveal must remain plausible.

## C08 — The Mismatched Shoes
**Strengths:** simple, warm, highly visual, strong friendship behavior, works muted.  
**Risks:** same shoe size must feel believable; avoid exaggerated bullying.

## C09 — The Missed Call
**Strengths:** highest relatability, avoids death-bait, gentle second-chance ending.  
**Risks:** weaker opening action; plant payoff must feel emotionally specific.

## C10 — The Last Clean Shirt
**Strengths:** reciprocal sacrifice, grounded family stakes.  
**Risks:** needs fast setup of two important appointments; lower curiosity than the top tier.
