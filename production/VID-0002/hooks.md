# VID-0002 — Hook Options

**Concept:** The Height Marks  
**Target duration:** 32–38s  
**Goal:** Create immediate emotional conflict without revealing the landlord's intent.

## H1 — Immediate conflict — SELECTED

- **Hook type:** immediate conflict / visual mystery
- **First visible frame:** Tight 9:16 shot of pencil height marks on a doorway. Maya's small hand is pressed flat over them. A metal scraper/trim tool approaches from the opposite side.
- **First action:** Maya jerks her hand tighter against the marks and looks up at the landlord.
- **Spoken line:** MAYA: "Don't erase them."
- **Unanswered question:** Why is the landlord removing the family's childhood height marks?
- **Promise:** The story will explain what he is actually doing with the marked doorway.
- **Risk note:** Tool must look like trim-removal work, not a threatening object.

## H2 — Visual mystery

- **Hook type:** visual mystery
- **First visible frame:** Extreme close-up of dated pencil marks beside a scraper blade already wedged under the painted trim.
- **First action:** The trim flexes slightly away from the wall.
- **Spoken line:** None.
- **Unanswered question:** Why is someone removing this part of the doorway during a move?
- **Promise:** The marked strip's fate will be revealed.
- **Risk note:** Less immediate human emotion unless Maya's reaction enters by second 2.

## H3 — Emotional statement

- **Hook type:** emotional statement
- **First visible frame:** Maya traces the highest pencil line while boxes pass behind her.
- **First action:** A tool enters frame and begins working below the marks.
- **Spoken line / VO:** "That wall knew every birthday."
- **Unanswered question:** Is the family about to lose the record permanently?
- **Promise:** The physical memory will become central to the ending.
- **Risk note:** More poetic, but weaker muted-first-frame comprehension than H1.

## H4 — Contradiction

- **Hook type:** contradiction
- **First visible frame:** Nearly empty apartment, but Maya is guarding a battered strip of doorway as if it is valuable.
- **First action:** Her father calls from off-screen, but she refuses to leave the doorway.
- **Spoken line:** FATHER (O.S.): "Maya, last box."
- **Unanswered question:** Why does this ordinary marked trim matter so much?
- **Promise:** The story will reveal its meaning and threat.
- **Risk note:** Conflict starts slower than H1.

## H5 — Dialogue

- **Hook type:** dialogue
- **First visible frame:** Landlord kneels by the trim, tool in hand; Maya enters quickly.
- **First action:** She steps between him and the doorway.
- **Spoken line:** MAYA: "You can't do that."
- **Unanswered question:** What is he doing, and why is she so upset?
- **Promise:** The dispute will be resolved visually.
- **Risk note:** Slightly generic until the marks are clearly visible.

## H6 — Unresolved outcome

- **Hook type:** unresolved outcome
- **First visible frame:** A long painted strip with handwritten height marks is half-separated from the doorway.
- **First action:** Maya grabs the loose end before it comes free.
- **Spoken line:** None.
- **Unanswered question:** Is she stopping destruction or interrupting something else?
- **Promise:** The meaning of the removal will be clarified.
- **Risk note:** Starts too close to the reveal and may weaken payoff surprise.

## H7 — Shocking action, restrained

- **Hook type:** shocking action
- **First visible frame:** The landlord pushes a scraper behind the height-marked trim.
- **First action:** Maya slaps her palm against the strip to stop him.
- **Spoken line:** MAYA: "Stop!"
- **Unanswered question:** Why is he tearing out something she desperately wants preserved?
- **Promise:** His intention will make sense by the payoff.
- **Risk note:** Must avoid melodramatic performance or unsafe hand/tool proximity.

## Ranked top 3

1. **H1 — Immediate conflict / visual mystery**
   - Best balance of muted comprehension, emotional specificity, conflict, and payoff preservation.
2. **H2 — Pure visual mystery**
   - Strongest no-dialogue option, but emotionally colder in the opening instant.
3. **H3 — Emotional statement**
   - Strong emotional framing, but relies more on audio and poetic language.

## Selected hook

**H1**

Reason: the first frame contains the story's emotional object, the threat, and the protagonist's resistance at once. It creates one clear question and remains understandable without audio.
