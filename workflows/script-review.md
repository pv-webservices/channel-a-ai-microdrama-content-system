# Script Review

Review after Micro-Drama Writer and Retention Optimizer.

Check:
- hook promise matches payoff,
- conflict appears early enough for target duration,
- motivation is coherent,
- visible action carries major information,
- no redundant narration,
- escalation is causal,
- payoff is earned,
- ending does not lecture,
- duration is plausible,
- originality policy passes.

Decision: `APPROVE`, `REVISE`, or `REJECT`.
