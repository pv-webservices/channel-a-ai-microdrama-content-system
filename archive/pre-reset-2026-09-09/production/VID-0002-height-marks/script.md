# VID-0002 — Script v1

**Story ID:** STORY-0002  
**Working title:** The Height Marks  
**Selected hook:** H1 — immediate conflict / visual mystery  
**Target duration:** ~35 seconds  
**Voice mode:** dialogue-led with no narrator  
**Status:** DRAFT — requires retention review and human script approval

## 1. Story objective

Make an apparently destructive act toward a family's childhood memories resolve into a practical act of preservation, with the meaning carried primarily by the doorway, the tool, and the intact marked strip rather than exposition.

## 2. Beat structure

| Beat | Time | Story change |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | Maya physically protects the height marks from the landlord's tool. |
| Situation | 3–7s | The empty apartment and dated marks establish that the family is moving and the marks cover years. |
| Conflict | 7–12s | The landlord says the apartment must be repaired; Maya insists the marks cannot be erased. |
| Escalation | 12–18s | Her father calls that the car is packed. Maya must leave, but the landlord resumes work at the marked trim. |
| Turning point | 18–24s | Maya realizes the tool is going behind the trim, not scraping the pencil away; the whole strip starts separating intact. |
| Payoff | 24–30s | The landlord removes the complete marked strip and reveals fresh replacement molding beside him. |
| Resolution | 30–35s | He lays the preserved strip across the final moving box; Maya touches the oldest mark and carries it out. |

## 4. Approximate spoken word count

30 spoken words including the off-screen father's line.

## 5. Continuity requirements

- Height marks must remain identical in position, handwriting style, dates, and spacing across every shot.
- The marked section is a **narrow removable vertical trim/molding strip**, not structural wall material.
- Tool enters behind the side edge of the trim; it never scrapes directly across the pencil writing.
- Landlord stops tool movement when Maya's hand enters the work area.
- Fresh replacement trim must be subtly visible before or during payoff so the repair logic is credible.
- Apartment remains nearly empty throughout; moving boxes should decrease toward the doorway, not multiply between shots.
- Maya's clothing/appearance will be locked during Character Consistency stage after script approval.

## 6. Risks / open questions

1. **Technical readability:** viewers need to understand that the marked piece is trim/molding. Storyboard must frame the edge separation clearly.
2. **Safety framing:** Maya's hand cannot remain near a moving tool. The landlord stops, she withdraws, then work resumes.
3. **Sentiment control:** acting and music should remain restrained; no crying close-up is necessary.
4. **Text legibility:** only a few representative names/ages should be readable. Too much text will clutter the mobile frame.
5. **Payoff timing:** do not reveal the fresh replacement strip prominently before the marked trim begins separating.
