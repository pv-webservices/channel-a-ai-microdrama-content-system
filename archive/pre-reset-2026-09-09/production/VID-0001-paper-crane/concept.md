# VID-0001 Concept

**Story ID:** STORY-0001  
**Working title:** The Paper Crane  
**Estimated duration:** 38 seconds  
**Content pillar:** unexpected kindness  
**Emotional category:** suspicion → embarrassment → tenderness

## Premise
Nia, a student, notices the school janitor repeatedly collecting discarded handwritten notes and assumes he is reading students' private messages.

## Main character
CHAR-001 — Nia, 17, observant and protective of her privacy.

## Central conflict
Nia catches CHAR-002 collecting a crumpled note she threw away and confronts him.

## Emotional trigger
Her discarded note contains a vulnerable sentence she never intended anyone to keep.

## Twist / payoff
The janitor never reads the writing. He trims away the inked sections and folds the blank paper into small cranes for a children's ward donation box.

## Why viewers may care
The opening resembles a privacy violation, but the reveal changes the meaning of the same physical action without making either character irrational.

## Visual potential
Crumpled notes, gloved hands, a paper cutter, folding motions, and a box filled with cranes create a clear visual chain.

## Originality note
The conflict is privacy and misinterpretation, not wealth/status punishment or a generic karma reversal.
