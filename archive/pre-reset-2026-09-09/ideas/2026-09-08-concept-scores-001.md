## Ranked shortlist

### 1. C01 — The Height Marks — 8.8
**Key strengths:** Strong visual hook, unusually original emotional object, highly readable family stakes, earned payoff, limited locations.  
**Key risks:** The trim-removal action must be visually believable and understandable quickly.

### 2. C02 — The Missing Shoe — 8.3
**Key strengths:** Natural countdown pressure, clear conflict, strong friendship reversal, easy-to-generate sports visuals.  
**Key risks:** The teammate must appear to be actively repairing the shoe quickly enough that the audience does not ask why she simply did not explain first.

### 3A. C04 — The Yanked Backpack — 8.2
**Key strengths:** Best pure first-frame/action hook, nearly narration-free, excellent 20–25 second fit, clear visual payoff.  
**Key risks:** Lower emotional depth than the top two; escalator mechanics must be rendered accurately.

### 3B. C11 — The Cracked Recorder — 8.2
**Key strengths:** Highest emotional intensity, strong preservation-vs-destruction conflict, meaningful father/son payoff.  
**Key risks:** Requires the battery/memory-card repair to be visually understandable without technical exposition.

## Other notable candidates

- **C03 — The Fake Tip (8.1):** strong social conflict and shareability, but the performative-generosity theme is less original.
- **C08 — The Locked Bicycle (8.0):** highly visual and tense, but depends on a believable reason the warning was not communicated effectively.
- **C05 — The Covered Painting (7.9):** original setting and strong visuals, but the physical evidence of light damage must read quickly.
- **C09 — The Unclaimed Medal (7.9):** emotionally clean, but the gesture is easier to predict.
